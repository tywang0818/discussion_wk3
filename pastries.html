My Favorite Order

Pain au Chocolat

My favorite thing to order at a cafe is a pain au chocolat. It has a crisp, buttery outside and rich chocolate folded into the layers inside.

I like to pair it with a cup of coffee. The warm pastry, melted chocolate, and coffee make a simple and satisfying breakfast.
